Ensemble Mechanisms: Bagging vs. Boosting Model Architecture
How ensemble techniques combine multiple models for superior stability.
1. Bagging & Random Forest (Parallel Learning):
Trains dozens of independent decision trees simultaneously on random bootstrap samples. At each branch, it picks a random subset of features to ensure individual trees are decorrelated. Averages all votes to drastically cut model variance and prevent overfitting.
2. Gradient Boosting (Sequential Learning):
Builds trees one after another in a sequential chain. Each new tree focuses specifically on learning from the mistakes and errors made by the previous trees, actively minimizing bias on difficult-to-predict severe accidents.
