Academic Distinction: Unsupervised Clustering vs. Supervised Labels Key Principle

Clustering is strictly unsupervised: the algorithm is given zero outcome labels (severity is hidden during training). Instead, K-Means groups collisions based on feature space proximity (speed limits, visibility, road geometry, environmental conditions). Once clusters emerge, we profile each cluster to discover real-world operational archetypes.
