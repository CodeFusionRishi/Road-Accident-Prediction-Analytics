Target Variable & Class Imbalance Analysis Critical Machine Learning Concept

Target formulation: Accident Severity mapped into binary risk for emergency resource dispatch: Severe (Fatal + Serious = 1) vs. Slight (Minor = 0).
The real-world UK distribution is heavily imbalanced: Slight crashes comprise ~75.8% while Severe crashes comprise ~24.2%. A naive trivial model predicting "Slight" for every row would achieve 75.8% accuracy while failing 100% of severe injuries. Hence, models are evaluated on Weighted & Macro F1-score and ROC-AUC with class_weight='balanced' penalty matrices.
